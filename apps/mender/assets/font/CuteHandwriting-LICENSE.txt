Cute Handwriting
by Paula Tennet — https://fontmeme.com/fonts/cute-handwriting-font/

Listed by the distributor as free, tagged "free for commercial use". The font
carries no embedded licence text of its own; its copyright notice reads
"Created with the help of MyScriptFont.com / Copyright belongs to the Creator",
so the terms are the distributor's listing rather than a named licence.

It is shown in Mender as a hand the board can be written in. The file here is
the author's `CuteHandWriting.ttf` converted to woff2; no glyph has been
changed.

Note: the face covers ASCII only. It has no arrows, em dash, ellipsis or curly
quotes, so anything the board draws with those needs another face.
