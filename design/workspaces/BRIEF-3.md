# Workspaces, round three: designed, and at home

Read BRIEF.md and BRIEF-2.md in this folder (kit, data, answer sheet, GitLab facts, ban list
all still hold). This round overrides both where they differ.

## Where the last two rounds went wrong (look at the renders)
- Round one (kit/01f-windows.png, 02b-hand.png, 03b-sky.png): "way too busy and way too out of
  place". Loud metaphor objects (lit windows, a fan of cards, a glowing sky) that spoke a
  different language from the rest of the app.
- Round two (kit/07-rail.png, 08-head.png, 09-column.png): "you went completely the other side,
  basic and simple". Bare lines of text with nothing designed about them.

## The target
The polish of the APPROVED popups, which he loves: kit/r-settings.png, kit/r-agent.png,
kit/d-checkout.png (ref-checkout.png), kit/d-slash.png, kit/d-ask.png. Study them closely and
read r-settings.html / r-agent.html / d-checkout.html for how they're built. That is the app's
current visual language: real liquid glass (liquid.js), smoky night grey, soft inner cards with a
hairline, tiles, chips with a small status dot or pixel sign, segmented rows, Fraunces for names,
Figtree for body, Silkscreen only for tiny labels, gold only for pressable, a lamp-lit sheen,
generous radius and padding. The workspaces surface should look like it was designed by the same
hand on the same day as those popups: crafted, tactile, rich in finish, but calm in count and
colour. Not a metaphor object, not bare text.

Rules of restraint that still apply: at rest, at most one accent colour per workspace (its
posture or its worst GitLab fact), all-good is quiet, nothing flares or floods, the yard's three
columns keep their room, the rack top right stays clear, no usage tips. Glass can live at rest
now (he moved the app to glass on purpose), but one surface, not a pile of them.

Problems must catch the eye: ci failed (sore), needs approvals, comments to answer, wants you.
Use small pixel signs (px.js style, 9x9, extend as needed) for ci / approvals / comments, as in
the approved popups' rows.

Deliverables as before, with your prefix: rest, -hover (INV-71880), -open (the one you're in, in
full: ticket + MR). Full size look, crop on the surface, and compare side by side with
r-settings.png before reporting: if it looks less finished than that, it isn't done.
